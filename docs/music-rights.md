# Music in production videos

The owner reported that the opening titles and “What Is AI?” music were generated on Suno's free plan. A later subscription does not, by default, give earlier songs commercial-use rights: https://help.suno.com/en/articles/2425729 . Regenerate them while the paid plan is active, or obtain an explicit licence covering the original tracks. Do not backdate an attestation.

The published opening video `202610081600-what-is-ai-opening-titles` is held out of the site's video list. Its source/media files are preserved for re-editing. A replacement render should have a new id and the actual soundtrack credit. The existing three CC0 explainers remain available. This does not erase copies on external platforms or caches.

For future owner tracks, upload the audio to `music/owner/` (or `collage/music/owner/`) on the assets branch and add `rights.json` beside it. Each entry must name the exact audio file and its SHA-256 hash, generation time, **plan active when generated**, song URL, reviewer and credit. The renderer validates the record and hash. This is a recorded owner review, not independent proof of legal ownership.

Example structure (replace every placeholder with verified values):

```json
{
  "new-intro.mp3": {
    "commercialUse": true,
    "provider": "suno",
    "planAtCreation": "pro",
    "createdAt": "ACTUAL_GENERATION_TIME_ISO",
    "reviewedAt": "ACTUAL_REVIEW_TIME_ISO",
    "reviewedBy": "ACCOUNT_OWNER_NAME",
    "sourceUrl": "https://suno.com/song/ACTUAL_SONG_ID",
    "sha256": "EXACT_64_CHARACTER_LOWERCASE_SHA256",
    "credit": "Music: New intro, made by AI Broadsheet with Suno"
  }
}
```

PowerShell: `Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 -LiteralPath .\new-intro.mp3` (use the hash in lowercase).

Missing, malformed, free-plan or mismatched records exclude that audio. The fallback also needs a CC0 entry in `collage/credits*.json`; absent that, the video renders without music. No new Suno song is purchased or generated by this workflow.

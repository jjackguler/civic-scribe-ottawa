## Funding and Genie: practical fit, not an eligibility decision

- [Ontario eligibility](https://feddev-ontario.canada.ca/en/funding-southern-ontario/regional-artificial-intelligence-initiative-businesses-who-can-apply): Adoption >=3 years and >=5 FTE; Commercialization >=1 year, >=1 FTE in southern Ontario and Canadian AI at TRL6+. Incorporated business, IP rights and responsible AI required; fewer than 500 FTE overall.
- [Funding terms](https://feddev-ontario.canada.ca/en/funding-southern-ontario/regional-artificial-intelligence-initiative-businesses-how-funding-works): $125K–$5M, interest-free, fully repayable, max 50% eligible costs. At a 50% share, $125K support implies $250K eligible costs. Applicant financing must be evidenced.
- [Deadlines](https://feddev-ontario.canada.ca/en/funding-southern-ontario/regional-artificial-intelligence-initiative-businesses-when-apply): 24 Nov 2026 for proposed projects/costs starting before 31 Mar 2027; final 19 Jan 2027, 4:59 p.m. EST.
- AI Broadsheet's ordinary use of subscriptions alone is insufficient. A validated AI product for other publishers may be a more plausible commercialization proposal. Company age, FTE, IP, validation and matching funds remain unverified. No funding application submitted.
- [Genie 4](https://deepmind.google/models/genie/) is restricted early research access. [Project Genie](https://labs.google/projectgenie) is a separate access route; [Google's FAQ](https://labs.google/fx/fr-ca/faq) lists Canada, age 18+ and Google AI Ultra. Owner access is unverified; no subscription bought.
- Editorial inference: use Genie for labelled fictional prototypes/interactive explainers, keep Unreal for a controlled reconstruction tied to historical sources. Generated scenes are not evidence of past events.

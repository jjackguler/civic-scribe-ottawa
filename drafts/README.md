## Canadian tech leaders sign open letter urging more data centres built in Canada

*Thirty-five signatories gathered by policy group Build Canada say Canada risks renting computing capacity from other countries if it does not build its own, according to CP24 and CTV News.*

`g53r38` · infrastructure · 167 words · written 2026-11-27 05:23 UTC · held because: built from web research: the editor reads it first

**In 30 seconds**

- Build Canada gathered 35 tech leaders to sign an open letter.
- The letter urges Canada to build more data centres domestically.
- The signatories warn Canada could end up renting capacity from other countries.

Canadian tech leaders have put their names to an open letter that calls for more data centres in Canada, according to CP24. Build Canada, a policy group, gathered the 35 signatories (cp24.com).

CTV News reports that the signatories say other countries will build data centres if Canada does not, leaving Canada to rent capacity from them (ctvnews.ca).

### What we know, and what we don't

BNN Bloomberg reports that the letter says demand for more computing capacity is here and that data centres will be built. It cites a figure 16 times what Canada has now (bnnbloomberg.ca).

Canadian Manufacturing reports that the signatories cite documents the federal AI minister received in January (Canadian Manufacturing). Beloud notes that the brief announcement does not name specific projects or locations (beloud.com).

### Why it matters

The letter adds to a public argument over data centres, which face opposition in some places. According to BNN Bloomberg, it addresses water, noise and power bills directly (bnnbloomberg.ca).

The sources do not say what the letter would mean for households or workers. Readers should treat its figures and comparisons as the signatories' claims (bnnbloomberg.ca).

### Questions

**Who gathered the signatories to the open letter?** Build Canada, a policy group, gathered the 35 signatories, according to CP24.

**What do the signatories warn will happen if Canada does not build data centres?** According to CTV News, the signatories say other countries will build data centres and Canada would then have to rent capacity from them.

**Does the letter name specific projects or locations?** No. Beloud notes that the brief announcement does not name specific projects or locations.

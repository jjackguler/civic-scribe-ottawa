**In 30 seconds**

- Everful has raised a $48.5 million Series A round.

Everful, a sourcing startup, has raised a $48.5 million Series A round, according to TechCrunch and a PR Newswire release. (techcrunch.com; prnewswire.com) The company says it is building AI and data infrastructure for global commerce. (prnewswire.com)

TechCrunch reports that Everful was co-founded in 2024 by Sisi Song, a former Alibaba executive, and Kevin Han, who sold an earlier company to Amazon. (techcrunch.com)

### What we know, and what we don't

Both TechCrunch and the release confirm the size of the round. (techcrunch.com; prnewswire.com) The release says existing investors Square Peg and Lightspeed also took part. (prnewswire.com) TechCrunch says the company began as a jewelry wholesale business and became a multi-category, factory-direct sourcing platform. (techcrunch.com)

Everful says it now sends over 5 million items each month to retailers and brands across more than 60 countries. It adds that this happened within about two and a half years of its launch. (prnewswire.com) FourWeekMBA says it found no Everful press release. It says the round, the revenue target and the shipping figures reach it from the founders through TechCrunch. (FourWeekMBA) These figures are the company's own claims.

TechCrunch says Everful will use the money to grow in the U.S. and Europe, with the U.K., France and Italy named, and to invest in AI. (techcrunch.com) It employs about 70 full-time staff, 45 of them in research and development. (techcrunch.com) Song says big firms once hired teams overseas to source goods, and that AI lets Everful turn that team into software. (prnewswire.com)

### Why it matters

For retailers, the pitch is that sourcing from factories abroad may no longer need a dedicated overseas team. This is Everful's claim, not a tested result. (prnewswire.com)

# Hidden Stories of AI — series plan

Vertical explainers (60–75 s) in the `noir` look of `zine.html`: black-and-white ink,
light beams and rain, one red accent; the house "b" logo (`logo-b.svg`) on the mast and end card.
One field per episode, three or four names per episode. Everything is drawn in code:
no book covers, artworks, photos, film images or likenesses of real people.
Facts come only from each board's cited sources.

## Season 1 (boards in `boards/hidden-stories-0N.json`)
1. Literature — Mary Shelley, Karel Čapek, Isaac Asimov, George Orwell
2. Science — al-Khwarizmi, Isaac Newton, Albert Einstein, Katherine Johnson
3. Art — Harold Cohen (AARON), Obvious (Edmond de Belamy), Refik Anadol (Unsupervised)
4. Mystery — Antikythera mechanism, the Mechanical Turk, Clever Hans, ELIZA

# AI on Screen / AI on the Page — series plan

Vertical explainers in the `poster` look of `zine.html`: surreal crayon posters
(red field and watching eye, night town with lit windows, orange fog, cubist heads).
Everything is drawn in code. No film stills, posters, characters' designs or
likenesses of real people; titles, years and names only, plus short quotes.
Facts come from the board's cited sources.

## AI on Screen (films)
1. 2001: A Space Odyssey (1968) — HAL 9000 and the real 1961 Bell Labs song  ← pilot, `ai-on-screen-01`
2. The Terminator (1984) — Skynet and the fear of autonomous weapons
3. Blade Runner (1982) — Philip K. Dick's question: what makes a person?
4. WarGames (1983) — the film that reached the White House
5. The Matrix (1999) — simulation, and the ideas the film borrowed
6. A.I. Artificial Intelligence (2001) — the Kubrick project Spielberg finished
7. Her (2013) — falling for a voice, a decade before chatbots
8. Ex Machina (2014) — the Turing test as a thriller

## AI on the Page (books)
1. Frankenstein (Mary Shelley, 1818) — the first made mind
2. R.U.R. (Karel Čapek, 1920) — where the word "robot" comes from
3. I, Robot (Isaac Asimov, 1950) — the Three Laws
4. Nineteen Eighty-Four (George Orwell, 1949) — the watching machine
5. Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep? (Philip K. Dick, 1968)
6. Neuromancer (William Gibson, 1984) — cyberspace

Same pipeline as the zine reels: check stills, queue the slug in `voice-queue.txt`,
push to `zine-voice-run` for ElevenLabs, add an optional Suno track as `music/<slug>.mp3`.
